Notes for the bench
===================

This is a page to try the editor with. It is long on purpose: some of its
lines run past the edge of the glass so that wrapping has something to do,
and there are more rows than the page can show so that the scroll bar has
somewhere to walk.

Why this page exists
--------------------

An editor with nothing to read is an editor nobody can try. This one is
copied into the ground of the app the first time it runs, so everything you
write here can be saved without asking the prompt for a path.

What to try
-----------

  - Move the caret with the arrows and with a finger on the text.
  - Press Enter at the end of a line and watch the caret go down to the row
    that the break opened, instead of staying over the words above it.
  - Turn Word wrap off in the View menu and see the long lines run off the
    right side of the page.
  - Turn Read Only on and see the keyboard go away: a page that cannot be
    written has nothing to type into.
  - Swipe the text with a finger and read the row you are on in the line
    under the page.

A long line follows, which is here to be wrapped and to be walked with the
finger once wrapping is turned off in the View menu of the bar: the quick
brown fox jumps over the lazy dog, and then it does it again, and again,
because a line that is longer than the glass is the one case that tells
whether a row is the text or the box.
