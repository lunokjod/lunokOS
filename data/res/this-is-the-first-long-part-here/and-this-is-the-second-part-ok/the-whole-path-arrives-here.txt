This file lives three levels down on purpose.

Its virtual path is 100 bytes:
/res/this-is-the-first-long-part-here/and-this-is-the-second-part-ok/the-whole-path-arrives-here.txt

That is longer than the 96 bytes the extension selector used to keep and shorter
than the 128-byte ceiling of the file system, so it is the one path that shows
whether the selector keeps it whole. Every name on the way is 32 characters or
fewer, because the image builder drops anything longer (see docs/70).

On the board: "open" this path, answer the question with a number, and the app
has to print the whole thing. A cut path would start partway through, or lose
the "/res/" at the front.
